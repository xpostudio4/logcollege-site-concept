Each entry includes

  • A daily personal call to worship
  • A daily Psalm
  • A daily Scripture reading (a built-in annual Bible reading plan with an alternative Hebrew Canonical Order plan for Old Testament readings)
  • A daily confessional affirmation that covers the Westminster Standards over the course of the year
  • A daily written prayer
  • A daily promise of God

The Directory for Secret Worship is a 365-day liturgical guide for personal worship with the goal of vital piety and disciplined spiritual formation – a useful tool for pastors, seminary students, new Christians, and every believer with a desire for greater spiritual development. The Directory can also be adapted for family worship and includes additional written prayers for various occasions. Use the Directory's structured approach to make every day count in your walk with the Lord! Get in the Word, deepen your confessional commitment, transform your prayer life, but most importantly, grow in your relationship with God!
