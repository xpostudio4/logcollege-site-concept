Before your visit, please note the following information:

  1. LCS has partnered with CafePress to supply all of our merchandise. CafePress handles all aspects of the sale. This eliminates the need for LCS staff involvement allowing staff to focus on the essential operations of the seminary.
  2. Since CafePress handles all aspects of the sale, CafePress determines merchandise base pricing.
  3. LCS receives a small percentage of sales. However, this percentage is quite negligible and does not provide any significant financial support for the seminary. LCS merchandise is intended as a means to represent the seminary and is not intended as a means of financial gain. Please don't confuse merchandise purchases with financial contributions!

Enjoy!
