Overview

The ATS program is designed to help students develop and expand their knowledge in basic Biblical and theological disciplines. The ATS program consists of 60 academic credit hours and requires a high school diploma or GED for admission.

20courses
545lecture hours
8,968pages of reading
21written assignments

Totals are added up from the program guide; a few items list no length, so the real load is slightly higher.

Admission

Requirement: High School Diploma or GED

Every applicant must also be a communing member in good standing of a local church, present a letter of recommendation from a church officer, and propose a qualified mentor. Full admission requirements.

Program requirements

  • Complete the Comprehensive Final Exam
  • All written assignments should follow current MLA formatting.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.
