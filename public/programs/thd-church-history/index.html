Doctor of Theology in Church History

Overview

This robust doctoral program is designed to provide students with a distinguished level of scholarship in church history and emphasizes the providence of God surrounding the development and growth of the Reformed Faith. The program consists of 56 academic hours and requires a master's degree (consisting of a minimum of 60 hours) in a theological discipline for admission.

Admission

Requirement: Open to male students only; minimum 60 hour Master's degree in a field of theological study which includes 6 hours of Hebrew and 6 hours of Greek; applicants without the original languages must first complete the M.Div. language courses (BS500; BS501) as part of the degree program; M.Div or Th.M preferred for admission.

Male applicants only, as stated in the LCS admission requirements.

Every applicant must also be a communing member in good standing of a local church, present a letter of recommendation from a church officer, and propose a qualified mentor. Full admission requirements.

Program requirements

  • All written assignments (with the exception of the dissertation) should follow current MLA formatting.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.
