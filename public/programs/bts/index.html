Overview

The BTS program provides students with a strong Biblical and theological foundation for ministry as well as preparation for graduate level seminary programs. The BTS program consists of 60 academic credit hours and requires an associate degree or equivalent for admission.

19courses

Totals are added up from the program guide; a few items list no length, so the real load is slightly higher.

Admission

Requirement: Associate degree or equivalent

Every applicant must also be a communing member in good standing of a local church, present a letter of recommendation from a church officer, and propose a qualified mentor. Full admission requirements.

Program requirements

  • Complete the Comprehensive Final Exam
  • All written course assignments should follow current MLA formatting.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.
  • Complete the Comprehensive Final Exam. Contact the seminary to arrange a time for your exam.
