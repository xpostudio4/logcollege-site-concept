Overview

The MBS program provides students with a systematic study of the Canonical books incorporating Hermeneutics, Biblical Theology, and Covenant Theology. The MBS program consists of 47 academic credit hours that may be transferred into the M.Div. program. The program requires a baccalaureate degree (in any field) or equivalent for admission.

Totals are added up from the program guide; a few items list no length, so the real load is slightly higher.

Admission

Requirement: Bachelor degree (in any field) or equivalent; open to male and female students; 30 hours maximum transfer credit allowed; all courses will transfer into MDiv program.

Every applicant must also be a communing member in good standing of a local church, present a letter of recommendation from a church officer, and propose a qualified mentor. Full admission requirements.

Program requirements

  • Complete the Comprehensive Final Exam
  • All written assignments (with the exception of the thesis) should follow current MLA formatting.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.

Students must use the Request for Approval of Thesis Topic Form when submitting their topic for approval. The completed form should be emailed to info@logcollege.net. Students should not begin thesis work until the seminary administration has first approved the topic.
·

Your Master's Thesis should be no less than 100 pages, 1 inch margins, twelve-point type, Times New Roman font, double spaced.
2

Your Master's Thesis should be free of grammatical and syntax errors and should represent the caliber of work you have completed during your Master's Program.
3
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis must include a submission page, table of contents, and full bibliography.
4
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis must be submitted to LCS administration by your mentor (after grading) for final review and approval before you will be considered for graduation.
