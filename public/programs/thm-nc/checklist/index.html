BS690 Master’s Thesis
  • Students with a thesis completed for their previous master’s program are exempt from the thesis requirement provided that they submit a copy of their previous thesis to the seminary.
  • Students without a previous thesis must complete a thesis as outlined below: Your thesis topic must be directly related to the field of counseling. You must work closely with your mentor in developing your thesis topic and in working through the thesis. Thesis topics must first be approved by the seminary administration. Students must use the Request for Approval of Thesis Topic Form when submitting their topic for approval. The completed form should be emailed to info@logcollege.net. Students should not begin thesis work until the seminary administration has first approved the topic.
  • FORMATTING: Current Turabian
  • 1. Your Master’s Thesis should be no less than 100 pages, 1 inch margins, twelve-point type, Times New Roman font, double spaced.
  • 2. Your Master’s Thesis should be free of grammatical and syntax errors and should represent the caliber of work you have completed during your Master’s Program.
  • 3. Your Master’s Thesis must include a submission page, table of contents, and full bibliography.
  • 4. Your Master’s Thesis must be submitted to LCS administration by your mentor (after grading) for final review and approval before you will be considered for graduation.
6

Program requirements

  • All written assignments (with the exception of the thesis) should follow current MLA formatting.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.
