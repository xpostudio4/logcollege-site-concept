Overview

The D.Min. program provides pastors with advanced theological study and enables them to further develop their ministry philosophy and ministry skills. The program offers three areas of concentration: Reformed Theological Thought, Nouthetic Counseling, and Apologetics. The program consists of 15 core credit hours, 15 credit hours in the chosen concentration, and a dissertation (6 credit hours) for a total of 36 academic credit hours. The program requires a M.Div. degree and 3 years of subsequent ministry experience for admission.

Admission

Requirement: Male students with M.Div. and 3 years ministry experience subsequent to conferring of M.Div.

Male applicants only, as stated in the LCS admission requirements.

Every applicant must also be a communing member in good standing of a local church, present a letter of recommendation from a church officer, and propose a qualified mentor. Full admission requirements.

Program requirements

  • 15 Core credit hours + 15 Concentration credit hours + 6 hour Dissertation. Choose one concentration (Reformed Theological Thought, Nouthetic Counseling, or Apologetics) consisting of 15 hours of credit.
  • All written assignments (with the exception of the dissertation) should follow current MLA formatting. Lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, and all other stated assignments must be submitted to your mentor for grading.
  • All numbered assignments (lecture outlines/notes, summaries, chapter summaries, papers, etc.) must be submitted to your mentor for grading.

FORMATTING: Current Turabian
1
Assignment
Your dissertation should be no less than 150 pages, 1 inch margins (1.5 inch left margin), twelve-point type, Times New Roman font, double spaced.

Assignment

Assignment

Assignment
