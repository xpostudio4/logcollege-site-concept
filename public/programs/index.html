Academic standards

What each credit hour requires

The programs are free, but they require dedication and hard work. Standards are high, not for the sake of achievement but for the sake of worship.

Undergraduate

  • 15 hours of lectures per credit hour
  • 150 pages of assigned reading per credit hour
  • 10–20 pages of research per course
  • Comprehensive final exam

Graduate

  • 15 hours of lectures per credit hour
  • 300 pages of assigned reading per credit hour
  • 30 pages of research per course
  • Comprehensive final exam and a thesis

Doctoral

  • 15 hours of lectures per credit hour
  • 600 pages of assigned reading per credit hour
  • 40 pages of research per course
  • Dissertation
