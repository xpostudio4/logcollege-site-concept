The old Puritan apprenticeship model

Taught through mentoring

Each student studies under a qualified, experienced pastor within the ministry context of his or her own local church. The mentor guides the studies, grades every assignment, and helps the student apply biblical principles to life.

LCS does not seek to replace the local church but to serve it, providing curriculum and educational support for equipping the saints for the work of ministry (Ephesians 4:11–12).
