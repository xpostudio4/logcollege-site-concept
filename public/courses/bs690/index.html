BS690 · Biblical Studies

Master's Thesis

Department
Biblical Studies
Credit hours
6

As assigned in MBS, MTS

·
Assignment
You must work closely with your mentor in developing your thesis topic and in working through the thesis. Thesis topics must first be approved by the seminary administration. Students must use the Request for Approval of Thesis Topic Form when submitting their topic for approval. The completed form should be emailed to info@logcollege.net. Students should not begin thesis work until the seminary administration has first approved the topic.
·
Assignment
FORMATTING: Current Turabian
1
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis should be no less than 100 pages, 1 inch margins, twelve-point type, Times New Roman font, double spaced.
2
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis should be free of grammatical and syntax errors and should represent the caliber of work you have completed during your Master's Program.
3
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis must include a submission page, table of contents, and full bibliography.
4
Assignment
Your Master's Thesis must be submitted to LCS administration by your mentor (after grading) for final review and approval before you will be considered for graduation.
·
Assignment
Complete the Comprehensive Final Exam. Contact the seminary to schedule a time for your exam.
5 written assignments
