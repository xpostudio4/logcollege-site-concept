Assignment
Exam: Complete the Westminster Shorter Catechism Exam. The exam will consist of 100 blanks which have been placed within the text of the Catechism. The blanks must be filled with the correct word. Your mentor must contact the seminary to obtain the exam. The exam must be proctored by your mentor or church officer.
