Assignment
Write a 40 page paper focusing on a particular creed/confession, or the expression and treatment of a particular doctrine appearing throughout the creeds/confessions.
32 lecture hours · 2,972 pages · 1 written assignment

Required in

  • Doctor of Theology in Church History
All numbered assignments are submitted to your mentor for grading.

Study with a mentor, in your own church.

No tuition, no relocation, textbooks provided.
