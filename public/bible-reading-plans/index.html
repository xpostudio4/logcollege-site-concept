Bible Reading Plans

Reading and studying the Word of God is a major “means of grace” for God's people. These plans can help you stay on track in this important spiritual discipline.

One of the most beneficial things you can do to promote your spiritual growth is to prayerfully read and study the Bible on a daily basis. All plans will take you through the Bible in a one year period unless otherwise noted.

  • M'Cheyne's Calendar for Daily Bible Readings
  • Read at Your Own Pace Bible Reading Plan
  • Straight Through the Bible Reading Plan
  • Chronological Bible Reading Plan
  • Annual Chronological Bible Reading Plan by Dr. Benjamin Shaw
  • Historical Bible Reading Plan
  • The Bible in 90 Days Reading Schedule
  • William Gouge Bible Reading Plan

    Five chapters three times per day

  • Tabletalk Two Readings per Day Bible Reading Plan

    One OT and one NT passage each day

  • Four Daily Readings Discipleship Journal Bible Reading Plan
  • 52 Week Daily Genre Bible Reading Plan

    A different literary genre each day

  • A Bible Reading Plan by Denny Burk

    Canonical order with "catch-up" days

  • One-Year Bible Reading Plan

    Old Testament once and New Testament twice per year

  • Two-Year Bible Reading Plan

    Psalms and Proverbs four times per year

  • Three-Year Bible Reading Plan

    One chapter per day

  • 5 Day Bible Reading Program
  • 5 Minutes x 5 Days New Testament Reading Program
  • Legacy Bible Reading Plan
  • Professor Grant Horner's Bible Reading System
  • Redemptive-Historical Bible Reading Plan

    Key thematic passages

  • Thematic Bible Reading Plan
  • Daily Light on the Daily Path Reading Plan
